itemscope=""> <section class="article-container" data-stats-ve="35"><h1>Video Chapters</h1><div class="article-content-container"><div class="cc"><p>Video Chapters break up a video into sections, each with an individual preview. Video chapters add info and context to each portion of the video and let you easily rewatch different parts of the video. Creators can add their own video chapters for each uploaded video or rely on automatic video chapters. Chapters may appear in the transcripts. Creators can always opt out of automatic video chapters in YouTube Studio.</p>
  <div class="no-margin"><strong>Note</strong>: If you don’t yet have access to chapters, apply for <a href="/youtube/answer/9890437" rel="noopener">access to Advanced features</a>.</div>
<h3 class="align-center"><a href="//www.youtube.com/watch?v=b1Fo_M_tj6w" data-videoid="b1Fo_M_tj6w" class="embedded-video-large"><span>How to Add Chapters to Your Videos Using Timestamps</span></a></h3>
<h3>To add your own video chapters:</h3>
      <li>Make sure that the first timestamp you list starts with 00:00.</li>
      <li>Your video should have at least three timestamps listed in ascending order.</li>
      <li>The minimum length for video chapters is 10 seconds.</li>
  <div class="no-margin"><strong>Note:</strong> This option will override automatic video chapters.</div>
<h3>To use automatic video chapters:</h3>
  <li>Click <strong>SHOW MORE</strong> and under <strong>Automatic Chapters</strong> select <strong>“Allow automatic chapters”</strong>. By default, this box will be checked for all new uploads. You can also allow automatic video chapters in <a href="/youtube/answer/6249768" rel="noopener">bulk</a>.</li>
  <div class="no-margin"><strong>Note</strong>: Not all videos are eligible for automatic chapters, and not all eligible videos will have automatic chapters. If the channel has any active strikes, or if the content may be inappropriate to some viewers, the video chapters feature won't be available.</div>
<h3>To opt out of automatic video chapters for a specific video:</h3>
  <li>Click <strong>SHOW MORE</strong> and under <strong>Automatic Chapters</strong> unselect <strong>“Allow automatic chapters"</strong>.</li>
  <div class="no-margin"><strong>Note:</strong> You can also opt out of automatic video chapters in <a href="/youtube/answer/6249768" rel="noopener">bulk</a>.</div>
<h3>To opt out of using automatic video chapters:</h3>
  <li>Click the <strong>Advanced settings, </strong>uncheck "Allow automatic chapters (when available and eligible)".</li>
